Design notes. Gold marks human knowing: earned level beads, chosen names, Fen's suggestions, the flames of the family view. Cool blue marks facts: the together-line, section eyebrows, structural counts. Levels render only when earned or offerable — an unofferable level is simply absent (never "not yet" where there is nothing to reach). The family view carries no per-person numbers and no ranking words; brighter rows simply glow more. The chooser is calm and single-purpose: name list, one confirm, done — the keeper's confirmation happens elsewhere and never blocks. Fen's strip may sit under the Journal as in the games; his line there is data-aware and points to the nearest unmet person with content. Motion: aurora drift and fireflies only; nothing pulses for attention.
