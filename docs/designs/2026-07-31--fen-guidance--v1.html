Fen guidance v1

Fen becomes the herald of the games. Four surfaces, one engine. Nothing interrupts; everything invites. Approved directions from the keeper: Play-bud instead of an ambient strip on non-game pages; the clearing; rare whispers; the first walk.

§0 holds everywhere: present tense, no memory-language. Truth palette holds: gold = human-told, cool blue = facts. Fen herself owns a third accent — ember amber — used ONLY for her presence and her invitations, so her voice is never confused with either truth channel. Colour freely in the sky; keep the truths clear in the hand.

A · The Play-bud tree · globe · person

RESTING — bud only, lower-left

Behaviour. A small ember bud breathes in the lower-left — Fen’s colour, mirror of the ⊕ on the right. It occupies nothing: no strip, no companion, until asked.

Tap → the strip fades in, Fen walks in (walk clip, 2.6s linear), speaks one data-drawn invitation, offers two chips. Come along → the seeded game (person pre-loaded). Not now → she waves (wave clip) and leaves; bud returns. Second tap same visit draws the next invitation — never repeats the declined one that day.
Bud hidden on game pages (she is already there) and inside Contribute flows. 52px, ≥16px from edges, above safe-area.

The one place Fen always is. Night clearing, moonlit; the animated forest plate from the companion bucket as ground; fireflies drift (pure CSS, reduced-motion honoured). Fen sits centre (idle clip), stretches or sleeps if you linger.

Three lanterns = today’s invitations from the engine: meet (cool border — a fact-edge), story (gold border — a human gap), place (cool). Deterministic per player+day; “show me other paths” re-rolls once. Tap a lantern → seeded game or the Contribute gap.
Also holds the whisper toggle: “Fen speaks on her own — yes / only here.” (localStorage lf_fen_quiet).

C · Threshold whispers rare · dismissible · owned by the quiet toggle

A WHISPER — after closing a photo
🦊Grisha stands beside her in that photograph — do you know him?Come
⊕
Rules of restraint (hard): only at a closed-loop pause (photo closed, story finished, game ended); max one per session; never twice on the same page; auto-fades in 7s; a swipe dismisses; “Not now” anywhere mutes for the day; the clearing toggle mutes forever. Contextual: the whisper references what was just seen, or stays silent — no generic lines, ever.
Fen does not animate in for a whisper — the card carries her face crop only. Whispers are murmurs, not visits.

D · The first walk once · after the anchor is chosen

AFTER “I am Michael’s daughter”
Michael’s daughter! Then Amma is your great-grandmother — she’s here, you know. Come, I’ll introduce you.
Take meI’ll wander on my own
⊕
The warmest minute. Fired once, right after anchor confirmation. Fen walks in, names a real relation computed from the anchor (grandparent preferred, else parent-sibling, else nearest unmet kin via the Walk’s BFS), and offers a guided first game seeded with that person. Declining is honoured instantly and forever — free roam is the law. Completing it records the first knowledge event and lands in the Journal as the first line of the player’s walk.

E · The invitation engine lf-invite.js — one machinery for A–D

Inputs: player’s knowledge_events, anchor kin-BFS (extracted from lf-walk), artefacts with no narrator, places with no visits. Output: typed invitations {kind: meet | story | place, subject, line(lang), target-url-with-seed}, ranked: unmet close kin › unheard stories about known people › untrod places. Every surface draws from the same ranked pool, so Fen never contradicts herself between the bud, the clearing and a whisper. Lines are template+data, per language, §0-checked.
